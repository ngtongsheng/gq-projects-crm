# 12 i18n EN/BM/中文
## Goal
Text can be shown in English, Bahasa Malaysia or Chinese per person, with the demo's dictionaries in place, a toggle
component, and the sidebar translated for site supervisors.
## Context
- D10. Demo: BM `TT` dictionary and `tr()` (~2741), per-person default language, toggle in Log work; Chinese `ZHEN`
  dictionary (~1913) and `CLANG` toggle (default English) in Costing. Free-text fields stay as typed.
- `language` column on profiles from task 03. No i18n library: a typed dictionary per locale and a `t(key)` helper.
## Files to touch
- `src/lib/i18n/dictionary.ts`, `src/lib/i18n/bm.ts`, `src/lib/i18n/zh.ts`, `src/lib/i18n/t.ts` (+ test)
- `src/components/app/language-toggle.tsx` (new), `/dev/components` entry
- `src/components/app/app-shell.tsx` (nav labels through `t`)
## Steps
1. Convert `TT` and `ZHEN` into typed dictionaries keyed by the English text (as the demo does); missing keys fall back
   to English.
2. Locale: the person's `language`, overridable by the toggle for the session (cookie).
3. Sidebar labels through `t`; site role in BM shows the demo's BM labels.
## Acceptance
- `pnpm check`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` pass.
- Parity: sidebar outline for a BM site supervisor equals the demo with BM selected.
## Out of scope
Translating page contents (their loops).
